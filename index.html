Resources

  • playmahjong.io - Chinese International Mahjong free to play in the browser, with an easy to read scoring gude and how to play guide
  • Mahjiku - Scoring app for Hong Kong Mahjong. Lots of great info on their site, including comparisons between variants.
  • The Mahjong App - American Mahjong app, with high ratings. Teaches you how to play with a tutorial & suggested hands. Has the official NMJL card for scoring, or you can play with alternate scoring rules. Free to play against bots with ads, plus various paid options.
